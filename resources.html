From Insight to Action

Information only helps when it points somewhere.

I spent years drowning in information while my life fell apart. I read books, started programs, burned through therapists, and kept using. From the outside I looked functional. I kept working, paid bills, and maintained relationships. Inside, I was barely holding it together. Every system seemed to lead to another doorway, acronym, or waitlist. The options felt endless and somehow useless at the same time.

This hub is meant to make the next decision simpler. It brings together practical pathways for detox, treatment, trauma therapy, public health services, recovery tools, and the researchers and clinicians whose work can help the whole picture make sense.

Start with what is most urgent. Use what fits. Leave what does not. Come back when you are ready. The goal is not perfection. It is direction.

Need help right now?

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. For crisis support anywhere in Canada, call or text 988.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

If you reach out through the contact form, I'll collect your name, email, and message — nothing more. That information is used only to get back to you. It's not shared, sold, or used for anything else.

This site uses two analytics tools to help me understand how people find and use it. Google Analytics is only active if you choose to allow analytics cookies. Netlify Web Analytics runs quietly in the background using general server data — no cookies, no individual tracking.

A few third-party services help keep the site running — things like hosting and the contact form. Some of these are based outside Canada, so your information may be processed there. I've chosen providers I trust, but I want you to know.

Please don't share anything urgent or crisis-related through the contact form — it's not monitored in real time. If you're in immediate distress, please reach out to a crisis line or emergency services.

Questions about this policy, or want to see or correct something I have about you? You can reach me through the contact page — I'm happy to help.

Optional analytics: I use optional analytics to understand how people use the site, where pages lose people, and what needs improvement. You can decline and still use the site normally. If you decline, I may ask again later in case your preferences change.
